# lidang-perspective

A persona skill that answers career, tech-trend, immigration and education questions
the way 立党 (Li Dang, [@lidangzzz](https://github.com/lidangzzz)) argues in his public
writing and videos. I generated it with [nuwa-skill](https://github.com/alchaincyf/nuwa-skill)
from public sources in May 2026; the research notes are in `references/research/` and
the sources are listed at the end of `SKILL.md`.

It is my reading of public material, not anything endorsed by him, and it does not
give investment advice. The skill text is in Chinese.

## Example

```text
用立党的视角看：EECS 本科生现在该不该把时间押在 EDA 方向？
```
